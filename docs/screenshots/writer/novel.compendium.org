#+TITLE: The Tidewater Clock: compendium

* People
** Wren Aldous
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: character
:RECORD: character
:ALIASES: Wren, the keeper
:EYES: grey
:BORN: 1893
:ROLE: protagonist
:END:
Keeper of the harbour clock for three years. Counts everything. Afraid of heights and climbs the tower every morning anyway.

** Silas Marr
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: character
:RECORD: character
:ALIASES: Silas, the old man
:BORN: 1842
:ROLE: mentor
:END:
Clockmaker. Built half the clocks in the county. Has been winding the engine under the tower for forty years and has told no one.

** Jory Aldous
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: character
:RECORD: character
:ALIASES: Jory
:BORN: 1898
:ROLE: Wren's brother
:END:
Fourteen, all elbows, runs everywhere. Goes where he is told not to.

** Ada Penhallow
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: character
:RECORD: character
:ALIASES: Ada, the harbourmaster
:BORN: 1866
:ROLE: harbourmaster
:END:
Keeps two pencils behind her ears and forty years of ledgers in a damp cellar.

* Places
** Saltmere
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: place
:ALIASES: the town
:END:
A harbour town on a coast that is not quite Cornwall. One quay, one breakwater, one clock.

** The clock tower
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: place
:ALIASES: the tower
:STEPS: 112
:END:
One hundred and twelve stone steps. The coal store at its foot hides the stair down.

** The breakwater
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: place
:ALIASES: breakwater
:END:
Half a mile of granite. The stones shiver when the tide turns.

* Things
** The tide engine
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: thing
:ALIASES: the engine, the drowned engine
:END:
Brass, the size of a cottage, with a green glass sphere at its heart. It keeps the sea's time, not the town's.

** The ledgers
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: thing
:END:
Forty years of tide readings in the Customs House cellar. Silas wrote /again/ in the margins.
