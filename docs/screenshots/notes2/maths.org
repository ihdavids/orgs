#+TITLE: Fourier series

* Definition
A function $f$ with period $2\pi$ that is square-integrable on
\( [-\pi, \pi] \) has the series

\[ f(x) \sim \frac{a_0}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left( a_n \cos nx + b_n \sin nx \right) \]

with coefficients

\begin{align}
a_n &= \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x) \cos nx \, dx \\
b_n &= \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x) \sin nx \, dx
\end{align}

* Parseval
The energy in the function is the energy in its coefficients:

\[ \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} |f(x)|^2 \, dx = \frac{a_0^2}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left( a_n^2 + b_n^2 \right) \]

* The Basel problem
For $f(x) = x$ the coefficients are $b_n = \frac{2(-1)^{n+1}}{n}$, and
Parseval gives Euler's sum
