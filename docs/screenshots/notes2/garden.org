#+TITLE: The allotment

* Plan for spring
  Beds one and two get [[id:4f7c2a90-compost-heap][the compost from last autumn]]
  before anything goes in. The brassicas move to bed three this year, as
  [[*Crop rotation][the rotation]] says, and the beans go where the potatoes
  were. Order seed potatoes by the end of January (see [[*Seed order]]).

  Water in the mornings; the notes on [[*Watering]] say why.

* Crop rotation
  Four beds, moving one along each year, so nothing grows where its own
  family grew the year before.

  - Bed 1: potatoes, then legumes
  - Bed 2: legumes, then brassicas
  - Bed 3: brassicas, then roots
  - Bed 4: roots, then potatoes

* Compost heap
  :PROPERTIES:
  :ID:       4f7c2a90-compost-heap
  :END:
  Two bays, turned every three weeks. Greens and browns roughly half and
  half by volume: grass and peelings, then cardboard and leaves.

  The left bay was started in September and should be ready by March. It
  smelled sour in October - too wet; more cardboard fixed it. A pH of
  6.8 from [[*Soil test, March][the March soil test]] means no lime is needed.

* Soil test, March
  Bought a kit from the garden centre; four samples, one per bed.

  | Bed |  pH | N      | P      | K      |
  |-----+-----+--------+--------+--------|
  |   1 | 6.8 | low    | medium | high   |
  |   2 | 6.5 | medium | medium | medium |
  |   3 | 7.0 | low    | high   | medium |
  |   4 | 6.6 | medium | low    | medium |

  Bed one needs nitrogen: green manure over winter, then compost.

* Watering
  Mornings, at the roots, not on the leaves: wet leaves overnight bring
  blight. A good soak twice a week beats a sprinkle every day.
