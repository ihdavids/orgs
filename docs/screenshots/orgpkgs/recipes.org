* Weeknight Chicken Curry
  :PROPERTIES:
  :source-url: https://spiceandsimmer.example/chicken-curry/
  :servings:   4
  :prep-time:  15 min
  :cook-time:  25 min
  :ready-in:   40 min
  :IMAGE:      [[file:images/recipes/chicken-curry.svg]]
  :RECORD:     recipe
  :END:
  A creamy, mild curry that's on the table in 40 minutes. Good with rice or warm naan.

** Ingredients
   - 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
   - 2 medium onions, finely chopped
   - 4 cloves garlic, grated
   - 1 tablespoon grated ginger
   - 2 tablespoons curry paste
   - 2 pounds boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
   - 1 (14 ounce) can coconut milk
   - 1 cup crushed tomatoes
   - 1/2 teaspoon salt
   - 2 handfuls baby spinach
   - a small bunch of coriander, to serve

** Directions
   1. Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onions and cook until soft and golden, about 8 minutes.
   2. Stir in the garlic, ginger and curry paste and cook for a minute, until fragrant.
   3. Add the chicken and stir until coated and no longer pink on the outside.
   4. Pour in the coconut milk and tomatoes, add the salt and simmer for 15 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
   5. Stir in the spinach until it wilts. Scatter with coriander and serve.

* Lemon Ricotta Pancakes
  :PROPERTIES:
  :servings:   2
  :prep-time:  10 min
  :cook-time:  15 min
  :ready-in:   25 min
  :IMAGE:      [[file:images/recipes/pancakes.svg]]
  :RECORD:     recipe
  :END:
  Saturday breakfast. Light and a little tangy; blueberries on top.

** Ingredients
   - 3/4 cup flour
   - 1 tablespoon sugar
   - 1 teaspoon baking powder
   - a pinch of salt
   - 1/2 cup ricotta
   - 1/2 cup milk
   - 1 egg, separated
   - zest of 1 lemon
   - butter, for the pan
   - 1 cup blueberries

** Directions
   1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
   2. In another bowl, mix the ricotta, milk, egg yolk and lemon zest, then fold into the flour.
   3. Beat the egg white to soft peaks and fold it in gently.
   4. Fry spoonfuls in a buttered pan over medium heat, 2 minutes a side. Serve with the blueberries.

* Shakshuka
  :PROPERTIES:
  :source-url: https://thegreenpan.example/shakshuka
  :servings:   3
  :prep-time:  10 min
  :cook-time:  25 min
  :ready-in:   35 min
  :IMAGE:      [[file:images/recipes/shakshuka.svg]]
  :RECORD:     recipe
  :END:
  Eggs poached in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce. One pan, lots of bread.

** Ingredients
   - 2 tablespoons olive oil
   - 1 onion, sliced
   - 2 red peppers, sliced
   - 2 cloves garlic, sliced
   - 1 teaspoon ground cumin
   - 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
   - 1 (28 ounce) can whole tomatoes
   - 6 eggs
   - 100 g feta
   - parsley and crusty bread, to serve

** Directions
   1. Soften the onion and peppers in the oil over medium heat, about 10 minutes.
   2. Add the garlic and spices and cook for a minute.
   3. Tip in the tomatoes, crush them with a spoon and simmer until thick, about 10 minutes.
   4. Make six hollows, crack an egg into each, cover and cook until the whites are set.
   5. Crumble over the feta and parsley and bring the pan to the table.

* Roast Squash and Lentil Salad
  :PROPERTIES:
  :servings:   4
  :prep-time:  15 min
  :cook-time:  35 min
  :ready-in:   50 min
  :IMAGE:      [[file:images/recipes/squash-salad.svg]]
  :RECORD:     recipe
  :END:
  Warm or cold, and better the next day in a lunchbox.

** Ingredients
   - 1 butternut squash, peeled and cut into chunks
   - 3 tablespoons olive oil
   - 1 cup green lentils
   - 2 handfuls rocket
   - 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
   - 50 g toasted pumpkin seeds
   - 1 tablespoon maple syrup
   - 2 tablespoons cider vinegar

** Directions
   1. Roast the squash with 1 tablespoon of the oil at 200°C for 35 minutes, turning once.
   2. Meanwhile simmer the lentils in plenty of water until just tender, about 20 minutes. Drain.
   3. Whisk the rest of the oil with the maple syrup and vinegar.
   4. Toss everything together while the squash is still warm.
