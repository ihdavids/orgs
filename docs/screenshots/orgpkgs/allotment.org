* The plot
  Ten metres by five, facing south, with the compost bins at the north end.
** Beds and rotation
   Four beds, moved round one place each year so nothing grows in the same
   soil two years running.

   | Bed | This year       | Next year       |
   |-----+-----------------+-----------------|
   |   1 | Potatoes        | Beans and peas  |
   |   2 | Beans and peas  | Brassicas       |
   |   3 | Brassicas       | Roots and onions |
   |   4 | Roots and onions | Potatoes        |
** Water and the shed
   The trough by the gate is shared; fill the cans before 9 on hot days.
   The shed key is on the hook behind the door, code 4-1-7.

* Sowing calendar
** Spring
   Broad beans and peas under fleece in March; first earlies in at Easter.
** Summer
   Successional lettuce every three weeks; French beans until July.
** Autumn and winter
   Garlic and overwintering onions in October, then mulch the empty beds.

* Pests and problems
  Slugs: beer traps and a torch walk after rain. Pigeons: net the brassicas.

* People
  Maureen on plot 12 knows everything about leeks. The committee meets on
  the first Saturday of the month.
